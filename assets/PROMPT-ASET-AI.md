# Prompt Aset Gambar AI — NalarSTEM

Pakai di Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram, DALL·E/ChatGPT, Gemini (Imagen), atau Bing Image Creator.
Simpan hasilnya ke folder `assets/img/` dengan **nama berkas persis seperti di bawah** (format .webp / .png / .jpg).

## Aturan agar seragam
1. **Tempelkan "GAYA DASAR" di akhir setiap prompt** supaya semua gambar terlihat satu keluarga.
2. **Jangan minta tulisan di gambar** — AI hampir selalu salah mengeja. Teks tetap ditampilkan oleh aplikasi.
3. Bila alatnya punya kolom *negative prompt*, isi dengan "NEGATIF" di bawah.
4. Buat 3–4 variasi tiap prompt, pilih yang paling bersih dan warnanya paling cocok.
5. Pakai *seed* / *style reference* yang sama (fitur "style reference" di Midjourney/Leonardo) dari gambar pertama yang kamu suka, agar gambar berikutnya senada.

### GAYA DASAR (tempel di akhir setiap prompt)
```
Flat vector illustration with soft gradients and subtle grain texture, friendly educational style for Indonesian junior high school students, clean rounded shapes, soft shadows, limited color palette of teal (#0d9488), deep ocean blue (#0369a1), warm amber (#f59e0b) and soft cream background (#f6f8f7), bright and optimistic, uncluttered composition with generous empty space, high detail, professional children's science book illustration, no text, no letters, no watermark
```

### NEGATIF
```
text, letters, words, watermark, logo, signature, blurry, low quality, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, scary, dark, photorealistic, cluttered background, western school uniform
```

Catatan tokoh: murid SMP Indonesia memakai seragam **kemeja putih dan celana/rok biru dongker**, sebagian murid perempuan berjilbab putih. Latar pesisir Jawa Timur (pantai, tambak garam, perahu nelayan, sawah).

## 1. Gambar utama beranda — `hero.webp` (16:9, ± 1600×900)
```
Wide hero illustration: a diverse group of four Indonesian junior high school students (white shirts, navy blue uniform, one girl wearing a white hijab) excitedly building science projects together on a sunny coastal schoolyard in East Java — one holds a small water bottle rocket, one adjusts a mini wind turbine, one tests a popsicle-stick bridge, one pours water through a homemade bottle water filter. Leave the left third of the image calm and less detailed for headline text.
```
