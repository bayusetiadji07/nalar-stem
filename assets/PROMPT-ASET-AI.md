# Prompt Aset Gambar AI — NalarSTEM

Pakai di Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram, DALL·E/ChatGPT, Gemini (Imagen), atau Bing Image Creator.
Simpan hasilnya ke folder `assets/img/` dengan **nama berkas persis seperti di bawah** (format .webp / .png / .jpg).

## Aturan agar seragam
1. **Tempelkan "GAYA DASAR" di akhir setiap prompt** supaya semua gambar terlihat satu keluarga.
2. **Jangan minta tulisan di gambar** — AI hampir selalu salah mengeja. Teks tetap ditampilkan oleh aplikasi.
3. Bila alatnya punya kolom *negative prompt*, isi dengan "NEGATIF" di bawah.
4. Buat 3–4 variasi tiap prompt, pilih yang paling bersih dan warnanya paling cocok.
5. Pakai *seed* / *style reference* yang sama (fitur "style reference" di Midjourney/Leonardo) dari gambar pertama yang kamu suka, agar gambar berikutnya senada.

### GAYA DASAR (tempel di akhir setiap prompt)
```
Flat vector illustration with soft gradients and subtle grain texture, friendly educational style for Indonesian junior high school students, clean rounded shapes, soft shadows, limited color palette of teal (#0d9488), deep ocean blue (#0369a1), warm amber (#f59e0b) and soft cream background (#f6f8f7), bright and optimistic, uncluttered composition with generous empty space, high detail, professional children's science book illustration, no text, no letters, no watermark
```

### NEGATIF
```
text, letters, words, watermark, logo, signature, blurry, low quality, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, scary, dark, photorealistic, cluttered background, western school uniform
```

Catatan tokoh: murid SMP Indonesia memakai seragam **kemeja putih dan celana/rok biru dongker**, sebagian murid perempuan berjilbab putih. Latar pesisir Jawa Timur (pantai, tambak garam, perahu nelayan, sawah).

## 1. Gambar utama beranda — `hero.webp` (16:9, ± 1600×900)
```
Wide hero illustration: a diverse group of four Indonesian junior high school students (white shirts, navy blue uniform, one girl wearing a white hijab) excitedly building science projects together on a sunny coastal schoolyard in East Java — one holds a small water bottle rocket, one adjusts a mini wind turbine, one tests a popsicle-stick bridge, one pours water through a homemade bottle water filter. In the background: the sea, fishing boats, salt ponds and a small school building. Floating around them are soft glowing icons of a lightbulb, gears, a magnifying glass, a leaf and a graph, suggesting curiosity and critical thinking. Leave the left third of the image calm and less detailed for headline text.
```

```
Character portrait of "Kak Nala", a warm and friendly young Indonesian female science tutor mascot, about 25 years old, wearing a teal hijab and a white lab coat over a teal blouse, round glasses, kind smile, holding a small glowing tablet with a lightbulb icon, gentle curious expression as if asking a thoughtful question. ## 3. Sampul misi (4:3, ± 1200×900) — satu gambar per misi

```
Illustration of a coastal Indonesian village street flooded by a high tide (rob), with salt ponds in the background. ```
Cross-section style illustration of a homemade water filter made from an upside-down clear plastic bottle, with clearly separated colorful layers of gravel, palm fiber, fine sand and charcoal; muddy brown river water poured on top slowly turns into clear water dripping into a glass below. A village river and a dry well in the soft background, a student observing closely with a notebook.
```

### `misi-pendingin.webp` — Kotak Pendingin Ikan Nelayan

Illustration of a traditional Indonesian fishing boat at dawn on the sea; in the foreground an open insulated cooler box with thick white walls filled with ice cubes and fresh fish. Soft red wavy arrows of heat outside the box are blocked by the thick walls, cool blue glow inside. A friendly fisherman and a student comparing two boxes.
```

### `misi-hidroponik.webp` — Kebun Sawi Hidroponik Sekolah

Illustration of an indoor school hydroponic garden: rows of bright green bok choy (sawi) growing in white net pots on a nutrient water tank, lit by purple-pink LED grow lights. Tiny floating CO2 bubbles and oxygen bubbles, a student measuring plant height with a ruler and another holding a TDS meter. Fresh, clean, hopeful atmosphere.
```

### `misi-roket.webp` — Roket Cuka & Soda Kue
```
Illustration of a small homemade bottle rocket with cardboard fins launching from a simple wooden stand on a green school field, bubbly foam and a burst of fizzing bubbles at the base showing a chemical reaction of vinegar and baking soda. A measuring cup, a spoon and a bowl of white powder nearby. Students wearing safety goggles cheer from behind a safety line. Target zone marked by colorful cones in the distance.
```

### `misi-roketair.webp` — Roket Air Bertekanan
```
Illustration of a plastic soda bottle water rocket with fins and a nose cone soaring high into a bright blue sky, spraying a powerful jet of water downward. On the ground: a launcher, a bicycle pump with a pressure gauge, and students wearing safety goggles standing behind a safety line, one holding the release string. Dynamic diagonal composition conveying pressure and action-reaction.

### `misi-jembatan.webp` — Jembatan Stik Es Krim
```
Illustration of a neat popsicle-stick truss bridge with triangular patterns spanning between two stacks of books, a small bucket hanging from its center as a load. Subtle orange arrows along the top members (compression) and blue arrows along the bottom members (tension). In the soft background, a real small village bridge over a rice-field irrigation canal. Students with glue bottles and clothespins.

```
Illustration of a small homemade three-blade wind turbine made from cut PVC pipe mounted on a wooden pole on a sandy beach at sunset, facing the sea breeze. ```

Illustration of a cozy wooden table in a simple Indonesian coastal home at night during a power outage: a junior high girl in a white hijab studies with a notebook under a small homemade lamp made of three glowing white LEDs on a cardboard board, wired with red and black cables, resistors and an AA battery holder. Warm soft light on her face, the rest of the room dim blue, a window showing a dark fishing village. Hopeful, focused mood.

Illustration of a school recycling station (bank sampah) in an Indonesian junior high school: two students operate a small homemade crane made of wood and popsicle sticks, with a copper-wire coil wrapped around a big iron nail hanging from the arm, connected to an AA battery holder and a switch. The electromagnet lifts a cluster of iron nails and a steel can from a mixed pile, while aluminum drink cans stay on the table; soft blue magnetic field lines around the coil. Sorting baskets labeled only with colors, no text.
